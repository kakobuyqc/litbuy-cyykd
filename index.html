Litbuy shopping guide

From an interesting link
to a useful shortlist.

A long list of Litbuy finds becomes more useful when every entry answers the same questions. Organize first, compare second, and check the actual listing before deciding.

01

Choose the category

Start with a specific need: walking shoes, a layering hoodie, a plain T-shirt or an accessory. Narrowing the category makes unrelated listings easier to set aside.

02

Record comparable details

For Litbuy product links or any other shopping list, record the URL, variant, measurements, listed material and date checked. Keep unknown details marked as unknown.

03

Check the complete picture

Review current photos, seller terms and a destination-specific delivery estimate. A low item price alone does not describe the final cost or the suitability of a product.

A practical comparison framework

Better notes.
Clearer decisions.

Use the same fields for each candidate. This turns a collection of screenshots into a shopping reference you can revisit.

  • Fit: compare garment measurements with something you already own.
  • Variant: record the exact size, color and option selected.
  • Evidence: separate seller descriptions from details visible in photos.
  • Terms: check returns, restrictions and the seller’s stated process.
  • Timing: save the date of each price, stock and delivery check.
Estimated delivered cost
Item price + domestic delivery + applicable service fees + international delivery + applicable taxes or duties. Confirm each component with a current quote.
What to inspect by product type
Category Useful checks
Shoes Size chart, foot-length guidance, outsole, upper material and intended activity.
Hoodies & sweaters Chest width, body length, sleeve length, fabric composition and care instructions.
T-shirts & jerseys Measurements, fit, fabric details, print placement and front/back photographs.
Jackets & sets Layering room, lining, closures and separate measurements for each piece.
Pants & shorts Waist method, inseam, rise, leg opening and stretch information.
Headwear & accessories Circumference or dimensions, adjustability, fastening, hardware and material disclosures.
Underwear Size chart, fabric composition, pack contents and hygiene-related return terms.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet generally refers to a curated list used to organize product links and shopping notes. Its contents depend on the creator. This page is an independent reading and category guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Litbuy service.

Where do the shopping links on this page go?

All shopping links lead to CAFANS. Category buttons open the corresponding CAFANS category. They do not open Litbuy checkout or confirm that a listing works with any particular shopping platform.

How should I compare product links?

Compare the exact variant, measurements, material description, current photographs, seller terms and estimated delivered cost. Save the listing URL and the date you checked it.

Do the brand illustrations verify product authenticity?

No. The illustrations are editorial style references, not product photographs, inventory records or evidence of authenticity. Check the actual listing and supporting documentation before purchasing.

How can I estimate the full cost of an order?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international delivery and any applicable taxes or duties. Use a current quote for the destination, weight and package dimensions.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent informational page that directs visitors to CAFANS. It does not claim affiliation with Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
